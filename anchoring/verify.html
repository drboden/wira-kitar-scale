Wira Kitar record check

Check that a recycling record has not been changed since KLEAN anchored it on a public blockchain. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

    How this works
    1. Each record's fields are put in a fixed order and turned into a SHA-256 fingerprint.
    2. Each day's fingerprints are combined into one "root" fingerprint, and only that root is written to the blockchain. No names, phone numbers or prices are ever published.
    3. This page recomputes the record's fingerprint and follows its proof up to the root. If any field has changed, even by 0.01 kg, the check fails.
    4. For Hedera batches, the page also reads the root straight from Hedera's public records to confirm it was published on that date.
