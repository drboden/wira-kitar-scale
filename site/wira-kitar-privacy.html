1. Who we are and what this covers

Janz Technologies Sdn Bhd ("KLEAN", "we", "us") runs the Wira Kitar programme, its WhatsApp service, dashboard and apps. This policy explains how we handle personal data about collectors, collection centre staff and owners, and buyers' contacts, under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), as amended in 2024.

  • For Wira Kitar registration, fee shares, traceability and reporting, KLEAN decides how personal data is used (we are the data user).
  • For each centre's own business records (prices, cash, advances), the centre is the data user, and KLEAN processes the data on the centre's behalf as data processor.
  • This policy is available in English and Bahasa Malaysia. You may rely on either version.

3. WhatsApp

You can register for Wira Kitar and receive your Wira Kitar QR ID on WhatsApp. When you message us, your messages pass through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, and Meta's own terms and privacy policy also apply to your use of WhatsApp. From WhatsApp we receive your phone number, WhatsApp profile name, the messages and voice notes you send, and the buttons you press.

  • We use these only to run Wira Kitar: onboarding, sending your QR ID, answering your commands (such as KOD, REKOD, ADUAN and BERHENTI) and replying to your questions.
  • If you are under 18 or do not agree to join, we do not register you and we delete your onboarding details within 24 hours, keeping only an anonymous count.
  • Your Wira Kitar QR code contains only your anonymised collector ID and a security code. It does not contain your name, phone number or vehicle plate.
  • We do not send you marketing messages. Send BERHENTI at any time to leave Wira Kitar and stop all messages.

4. Blockchain

To prove that records have not been changed, we write a daily fingerprint (a code calculated from the records) to the Hedera public ledger. The fingerprint cannot be turned back into the records, and it contains no names, phone numbers, plates, prices or payments. A blockchain entry cannot be deleted, which is why no personal data ever goes on it.

5. Where data is kept and who helps us

Data is kept on secure cloud servers run by our service providers, which include Meta (WhatsApp messaging) and our cloud database host. Some providers may store data outside Malaysia, including in the United States. Where they do, we require protection comparable to the PDPA.

6. How long we keep it

Weighing and sales records are kept for 7 years, for EPR and audit purposes. Personal contact details are deleted or anonymised within 12 months after you stop using Wira Kitar, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. WhatsApp message logs keep only what is needed for audit (time, type and button or command), not the full text of your messages.

7. Security

We use access by role, encryption, backups and audit logs. If a data breach is likely to cause you significant harm, we will notify you and the Personal Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, withdraw consent, ask us to delete it, or stop receiving messages. Withdrawing consent means we cannot continue paying Wira Kitar fee shares, but records already used in reports are kept in anonymised form. We respond within 21 days.

Deleting your data

To ask us to delete your data, send BERHENTI on WhatsApp, or email us at the address below with the phone number you used. We will delete or anonymise your personal data within 21 days, except records we must keep by law, which are kept in anonymised form.

3. WhatsApp

Anda boleh mendaftar untuk Wira Kitar dan menerima ID QR Wira Kitar anda melalui WhatsApp. Apabila anda menghantar mesej kepada kami, mesej anda melalui Platform Perniagaan WhatsApp milik Meta, dan terma serta dasar privasi Meta juga terpakai kepada penggunaan WhatsApp anda. Daripada WhatsApp, kami menerima nombor telefon anda, nama profil WhatsApp, mesej dan nota suara yang anda hantar, dan butang yang anda tekan.

  • Kami menggunakannya hanya untuk menjalankan Wira Kitar: pendaftaran, menghantar ID QR anda, menjawab arahan anda (seperti KOD, REKOD, ADUAN dan BERHENTI) dan menjawab soalan anda.
  • Jika anda berumur bawah 18 tahun atau tidak bersetuju untuk menyertai, kami tidak mendaftarkan anda dan kami memadam butiran pendaftaran anda dalam masa 24 jam, dengan hanya menyimpan kiraan tanpa nama.
  • Kod QR Wira Kitar anda hanya mengandungi ID pengumpul tanpa nama dan kod keselamatan. Ia tidak mengandungi nama, nombor telefon atau nombor kenderaan anda.
  • Kami tidak menghantar mesej pemasaran. Hantar BERHENTI pada bila-bila masa untuk keluar daripada Wira Kitar dan menghentikan semua mesej.

4. Blockchain

Untuk membuktikan rekod tidak diubah, kami menulis cap jari harian (kod yang dikira daripada rekod) ke lejar awam Hedera. Cap jari ini tidak boleh ditukar kembali kepada rekod, dan ia tidak mengandungi nama, nombor telefon, nombor kenderaan, harga atau bayaran. Rekod blockchain tidak boleh dipadam, sebab itulah tiada data peribadi dimasukkan ke dalamnya.

5. Di mana data disimpan dan siapa yang membantu kami

Data disimpan di pelayan awan yang selamat dikendalikan oleh penyedia perkhidmatan kami, termasuk Meta (pesanan WhatsApp) dan hos pangkalan data awan kami. Sesetengah penyedia mungkin menyimpan data di luar Malaysia, termasuk di Amerika Syarikat. Jika demikian, kami memerlukan perlindungan yang setanding dengan PDPA.

6. Berapa lama kami menyimpannya

Rekod timbangan dan jualan disimpan selama 7 tahun untuk tujuan EPR dan audit. Butiran hubungan peribadi dipadam atau dijadikan tanpa nama dalam masa 12 bulan selepas anda berhenti menggunakan Wira Kitar, kecuali jika undang-undang menghendaki kami menyimpannya lebih lama. Log mesej WhatsApp hanya menyimpan apa yang diperlukan untuk audit (masa, jenis dan butang atau arahan), bukan teks penuh mesej anda.

7. Keselamatan

Kami menggunakan akses mengikut peranan, penyulitan, sandaran dan log audit. Jika pelanggaran data mungkin menyebabkan kemudaratan ketara kepada anda, kami akan memaklumkan anda dan Pesuruhjaya Perlindungan Data Peribadi seperti yang dikehendaki oleh undang-undang.

8. Hak anda

Anda boleh meminta untuk melihat data anda, membetulkannya, menarik balik persetujuan, meminta kami memadamnya, atau berhenti menerima mesej. Menarik balik persetujuan bermakna kami tidak dapat terus membayar bahagian yuran Wira Kitar, tetapi rekod yang telah digunakan dalam laporan disimpan dalam bentuk tanpa nama. Kami menjawab dalam masa 21 hari.

Memadam data anda

Kami akan memadam atau menjadikan data peribadi anda tanpa nama dalam masa 21 hari, kecuali rekod yang wajib disimpan mengikut undang-undang, yang akan disimpan dalam bentuk tanpa nama.
